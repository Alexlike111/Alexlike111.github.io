Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy covers the games published by Ragdoll Lab (Katerina Ponomarova, sole proprietor, Ukraine; "we"): Office Smash: Beat the Boss and Stuntman Crash: Ragdoll Launch, and any future games that link to this page.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information. Our games have no accounts, no sign-up, no chat and no user-generated content. The game code does not send data to our own servers; we do not run any.

Game progress

Your progress (levels, coins, upgrades, settings) is saved through the platform where you play, for example YouTube Playables cloud save or the storage provided by the game platform. This data stays with that platform and is used only to restore your progress.

Advertising and analytics

Ads, if shown, are served by the platform where you play (for example YouTube or Playgama) under that platform's own privacy policy. The platform may also provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as number of plays and average session length. We never receive information that identifies you.

  • Google / YouTube: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Playgama: privacy policy at playgama.com

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Progress saved by a platform can be removed through that platform. For any privacy question write to alexlike15@gmail.com.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
