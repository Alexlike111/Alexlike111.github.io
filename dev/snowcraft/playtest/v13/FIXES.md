Everything below was reported by real-input players; fix the CAUSE, verify by playing (real input), keep all tests green.

## Group X1 — onboarding, goals, warmth (owner of: quest.ts, starter.ts, ui/devhud.ts goal part + HUD, overlay.ts, fixtures.ts stove/fuel parts, nearby.ts markers, draw.ts markers, lore/journal texts)
1. **Goal text must never disappear** (all 5 players: «Цель» pill only; the novice quit). Remove the 6-second collapse in devhud.ts/hudmode: the goal line (≤ 8 words) stays visible until the goal is done; on 'min' mode it stays too (smaller). A new goal flashes for 3 s.
2. **Coal «у дизельной» must be easy** (novice found coal 7 game-hours late, behind a wall; others found it at 4.8 gm). Guarantee: on a new game ≥ 3 coal piles lie OUTSIDE, in plain view, within 40 cells of the spawn on the walkable route to the station door, each with the item glint; the goal arrow (objectiveTarget) must point to the nearest REACHABLE coal (no wall between: check with the existing walkable/solid test, else the next one) and the HUD shows the distance. Auto-pickup radius must really be ~10 cells (explorer: only picks up when standing ON the pile; phone: 3 coal piles of 4.8 kg were not picked) — coordinate: the pickup code is in tools.ts (agent X2 owns it), so only the PLACEMENT/arrow is yours; tell X2 via the report if you see the radius bug.
3. **Door digging**: show a bright «копай здесь» marker/outline on the door's snow (the tile to dig) while the goal «Откопай дверь» is active and make the goal say how: «Откопай дверь лопатой (слот 2)». The goal also completes when the player enters the house another way (exists).
4. **Stove**: RMB/E at a stove with NO fuel in hand must automatically take the best fuel from the pack (coal > driftwood log > plank > fat) and put it in; if the pack has none say «Нужен уголь: подбери его снаружи». Toast 30 game-minutes before a stove/lamp goes out («Печка скоро погаснет — подкинь уголь») and when it goes out.
5. **Cold guidance**: when core < 35.5 °C and the player is outdoors and no fire is lit within 30 cells, the goal line is temporarily replaced by «Согрейся: печка в доме» (with arrow to the station door); when core < 34.5 add a subtle blue-white screen-edge vignette (CSS, ≤ 0.35 opacity, in overlay.ts) that grows as it drops, so the temperature number is not the only signal.
6. **After the keeper's log (goal «Построй иглу…» had no counter and no marker, 190 game-min of nothing)**: the igloo goal shows a counter «Блоков 3/10» (count blocks placed by the player outside the station; the quest already tracks `runStats.placed`) and an arrow to the lee snow field west of the station (objectiveTarget). Add 2 short mini-goals before the endless rotation starts: «Поймай рыбу или куропатку» (fish_caught / bird_caught events; arrow to the nearest hole/flock) and «Переночуй в тепле» (sleep in the shelter); each with a visible win toast.
7. **Sleep refusal bug** (novice): RMB near the stove hit the bed → toast «Слишком холодно спать» AND the clock jumped 22:47 → 00:17. A refused sleep must not advance time (player.ts startSleep belongs to X2; you only check the refusal toast text: «Слишком холодно спать: растопи печь»).
Tests: update quest/hudmode/simplify selftests; add onboarding.selftest.ts (goal text visible after 30 s, coal reachable, stove takes coal from the pack, cold goal override, counter).

## Group X2 — digging, pickup, inventory feel (owner of: tools.ts, inventory.ts, search.ts, player.ts, ui/bag.ts, engine/input.ts)
1. **Snow digging must give a result and never trap** (novice, fan, phone all stuck: shovel load hits a cap 26–32 units, then «Бросить снег», no text; knife says «Сначала откопай снег»; tooltip says «Мешает стена» while the preview says ok). Fix: (a) when the blade is full AUTO-TOSS the snow a few cells to the side/behind with the existing throw particles so digging continues without any extra step (keep the manual throw for players who want it); (b) every refusal shows a ≤ 4-word reason in the tooltip AND a toast the first time («Лопата полна»…); (c) tooltip, preview square and actual result must agree (find why «ok» and «Мешает стена» differ for the knife); (d) the first snow block is easy: a short guided path — knife on dense snow cuts a block in ~0.5 s, loose snow says «Рыхлый: утопчи лопатой (ПКМ)»; (e) digging loose snow gives a floater «−снег» feedback so the action feels productive (no inventory item needed).
2. **Tap-aim / far click gives no response** (phone, fan): clicking/tapping a tile out of reach shows «Слишком далеко» (tooltip + a quiet sound), never silence.
3. **Auto-pickup radius**: spec is ~10 cells; players report it only works when standing on the pile (2–3 cells away nothing; coal piles of 4.8 kg ignored although the pack is light). Find the bug (heavy flag? vertical range? age delay of world-spawned drops? `huntDrops`/sled exclusions?) and fix; add a test: coal at 8 cells flies in.
4. **Dropping**: G drops ONE item of the selected stack (Shift+G the whole stack); dropped items stay visible and lying on the ground (explorer/fan: they vanished after ~8 s while still in G.drops: find out, likely buried by snow or rendered under it — lift them on top of the snow); walking away > 3 cells and back picks them up (no 10-cell rule); toast «Брошено: Спички ×1».
5. **Sleep refusal bug**: startSleep() refused with «Слишком холодно спать» but the clock jumped 1.5 h: a refused sleep must not change time at all.
6. **Inventory tooltips** (fan): hovering an item in the belt/bag/crafting list shows name + one-line purpose (ItemDef.desc / itemName) + weight; HUD belt must NOT accept drag-drops while the inventory is open (items were thrown on the ground), the belt cells inside the inventory panel are the belt; key 7–9 without a pouch → toast «Нужен подсумок».
7. **Search time**: opening a chest/shelf with bare hands takes 12–42 game-min (~18 s real) = boredom. Make a normal chest 6 game-min (≈2.5 s), a frozen one 10; keep the progress bar and let the player walk away (cancels); gloves penalty halved.
8. **Phone 810×253 bag panel**: «По весу» overlaps the «Что умею» column and the second slot row is cut by «закрыть»: make the bag panel scroll inside its box and put sort buttons in the header at that size; second tap on the Сумка button closes it.
Tests: extend tools/inventory/search/controls selftests; add digging.selftest.ts (auto-toss, reasons, far-click message), drop tests.

## Group X3 — world content and pacing (owner of: locations.ts, extras.ts, hunt.ts, lore.ts, weather.ts, worldgen.ts only where listed)
1. **Clock speed** (fan: 2.3 game-min per real second; explorer 5 game-min per 60 frames; first night in 10 real minutes): halve the base clock speed (MIN_PER_SEC in weather.ts) so a game day ≈ 20 real minutes; every duration that is expressed in game-minutes keeps its meaning (fuel, hunger, tool times are tied to real seconds or game minutes — check each unit and keep the design intent: DESIGN.md says the first storm / first drift etc. are in game days: do NOT change those); update the tests that assume the old speed (use the constant), and report the balance effect (survival gets easier per real minute).
2. **Beach chest gets the ice chisel** (explorer: the ship deck chest «Крышка вмёрзла: нужна пешня» but the chisel is 1500 cells away at the mine): put an `ice_chisel` in the beach/ice chest on the way (x≈900–1100) AND add a line to the deck note «пешня осталась на пляже»; **tin cache at the old cairn (x≈183,y≈176)**: the goal «Найди дневник доктора в жестянке» must find a note there (it is empty): put the note in.
3. **Mine west trail** (explorer: dead end at x≈242,y≈276 with rubble and no hint): add a lit lantern/sign + a note «Завал — разбей киркой (она у портала)» or make the entrance steps visible; **ship deck** wall at x≈1762,y≈304 and cabin door unlabeled: label the cabin door and the ladder (tooltip «Каюта: дверь» / «Лестница»), and make the two bears on the upper deck not block the path to the cabin door.
4. **Ptarmigan hunting is unplayable by walking** (explorer: birds flush at 7–10 cells; strike reach 14): fix radii: walking flush at 8 cells, running 40, standing still never; strike reach 12 from the player; hint text «Подкрадись (иди медленно, не беги) и ударь».
5. **Ice drift** (explorer: carried 360 cells west in 114 game-min while standing on shore ice?): verify; if a player standing on fast ice near the beach can be carried that far, limit drift to pack ice only and make the tide-crack / drifting floe warning clearer («Лёд пошёл: уходи на берег»).
6. **Long empty walks** (explorer: 60–90 game-min between points): add 3 small things on the long legs (each with a tooltip and some use): a driftwood stack with 2–3 logs, a lost mitten/biscuit tin, an old campfire ring with a note, bear tracks leading to the ship (hunt.ts tracks), placed with extras.ts-style helpers at fixed x positions between the station and the mine (x 330–600) and between the beach and the ship (x 1100–1650). Keep them cheap; do not add enemies.
Tests: extend extras/world/hunt selftests; update fauna/weather tests for the clock change.
