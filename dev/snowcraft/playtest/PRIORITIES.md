# Playability priorities (owner feedback, 2026-11-07)
The owner's verdict: "the game is good but not playable and not interesting". Their top three pains (chosen from a list):
1. **Непонятно, что делать** — unclear what to do (objectives, hints, onboarding, next step, progress feeling).
2. **Неудобно управлять** — controls are inconvenient (PC and phone: aiming, slow/vague actions, getting stuck).
3. **Скучно, ничего не происходит** — boring, nothing happens (events, finds, threats, rhythm).
Not chosen: difficulty/balance numbers (still tune, but lower priority).
Rules for whoever implements: fix these three first; keep fixes small and solid; every change needs a test or a screenshot; keep it simple (DESIGN §16); no methane.
Analyst reports (Haiku swarm, read-only playtests of the v5.2 build): onboarding, pc-controls, phone-touch, survival-balance, objectives-endings, snow-building, world-exploration, living-world, game-feel, fuzz-bugs (*.md in this folder).
