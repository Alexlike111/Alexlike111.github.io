Smash Circuit - audio credits and licences
===========================================

All sound effects, the menu music and the race music in this folder are cut, layered and level-matched from the
following recordings. Details per file: docs/AUDIO.md in the source repository.

1. Kenney (https://kenney.nl) - Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, UI Audio, Foley Sounds, RPG Audio, Digital Audio,
   Sci-Fi Sounds, Music Jingles, Music Loops.
   License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Credit (Kenney or www.kenney.nl) is not required, but given here.
   Files: impact_*, crunch_*, debris_*, scrape, click, buy, error, countdown, go, finish, lap, pickup, nitro, boost,
   shockwave, oil, shield, shieldpop, repair, music_menu.

2. "Battle Theme A" by cynicmusic, https://opengameart.org/content/battle-theme-a
   License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).
   File: music.

3. Kenney, Starter-Kit-Racing (https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing), audio/engine.ogg and audio/skid.ogg.
   License: MIT
   File: engine, skid.
